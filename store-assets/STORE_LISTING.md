## Description — English (Localized)

🎴 Alias Quest — the party word game reimagined as an RPG adventure!

Describe the words on the parchment card without saying them while your guild guesses as many as possible before time runs out. Swipe the card right for victory or left to retreat!

⚔️ WHAT MAKES ALIAS QUEST SPECIAL:
• Unique RPG card game design — words appear on authentic parchment cards
• 1175+ words in Romanian
• 15 realms (categories): Animals, Food, Sports, Professions, Nature, Technology, Movies, Music, History, Geography, School, Home, Emotions, Clothing, and General
• 3 difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard
• Swipe gestures for lightning-fast gameplay

🛡️ FEATURES:
• 2-4 guilds (teams) with custom names
• Mission duration: 30s, 45s, 60s, 90s, or 120s
• Customizable victory score (25-100 XP)
• Optional retreat penalty (-1 point)
• Sound effects and haptic feedback
• Complete chronicles (statistics): wins, records, leaderboard

🏆 HOW TO PLAY:
1. Form guilds and choose your realms
2. One player describes the word on the parchment
3. The rest of the guild has to guess
4. Swipe right = Victory! Swipe left = Retreat
5. First guild to reach the score becomes Champion!

Perfect for parties, game nights, or family fun. No internet required!
