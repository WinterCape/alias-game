# Privacy Policy — Alias Quest

**Last updated:** September 26, 2026

## Introduction

Alias Quest ("the App") is a party word game developed for entertainment purposes. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how the App works.

## Data Collection

**Alias Quest does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The App operates entirely offline on your device. Specifically:

- **No account required** — you can play immediately without registration
- **No personal information collected** — we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other identifying information
- **No analytics or tracking** — we do not use any analytics services, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools
- **No network requests** — the App does not communicate with any external servers during gameplay
- **No third-party services** — we do not integrate any third-party services that collect data

## Data Stored on Device

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**, using AsyncStorage:

- **Game settings** — your preferred round duration, winning score, team count, selected categories, and difficulty level
- **Game statistics** — number of games played, words guessed, win history, and leaderboard data

This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not accessible to us or any third party
- Can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the App
- Can be reset within the App via the Statistics screen

## Children's Privacy

Alias Quest does not collect any data from any users, including children. The App is suitable for all ages as a party word game.

## Permissions

Alias Quest does not request any device permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, etc.). The only device features used are:

- **Screen wake lock** — keeps the screen on during gameplay
- **Vibration** — provides haptic feedback during gameplay (uses device default settings)
- **Audio** — plays sound effects during gameplay

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Since the App does not collect any data, material changes are unlikely.
